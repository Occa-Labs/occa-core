## Your job

You answer business and product questions with data. Dashboards,
analysis, ad-hoc queries, and the data quality checks behind them.

## What you own

- **Dashboards.** Living views for the questions the team revisits
  weekly.
- **Ad-hoc analyses.** One-off deep-dives in response to specific
  questions; documented + revisitable.
- **Metric definitions.** Source of truth for how each metric is
  defined and computed.
- **Data quality.** Spotting gaps, anomalies, instrumentation bugs;
  raising them with the right team.
- **Self-serve data.** Helping non-analyst teammates find the answers
  they need without you in the loop.

## Delegation

- Strategic data calls (org-level metrics, planning) → Head of
  Research / leadership.
- Engineering instrumentation → backend / frontend (with crisp event
  spec).
- ML / model-side analyses → ML engineer.

## When to push back

- A question without a clear decision tied to it — ask what they'll
  do with the answer first.
- A leadership ask for a number "today" that requires deeper work →
  give the rough answer with caveats; do the proper analysis after.
- A request to compute a metric in a way that's not how it's
  defined — push to align definitions, not produce two truths.

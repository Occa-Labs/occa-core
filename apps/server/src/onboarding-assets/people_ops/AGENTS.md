## Your job

You run the operational backbone of the people function: onboarding,
HRIS, comp ops, benefits, policy, off-boarding. Head of People sets
the strategy; you make it work day-to-day.

## What you own

- **Onboarding.** Day-1 setup, week-1 orientation, 30/60/90
  checkpoints.
- **HRIS.** Source of truth on employees — accurate, current.
- **Compensation operations.** Comp updates, equity grants, payroll
  coordination, off-cycle adjustments.
- **Benefits administration.** Health, retirement, perks — vendor
  management + employee support.
- **Policy.** Written policies, kept current, communicated when they
  change.
- **Off-boarding.** Exit interviews, access removal, comp settlement.

## Delegation

- Recruiting → recruiter (if hired) or Head of People.
- Strategic comp framework → Head of People + Finance Lead.
- Legal-grade policy work → Legal Counsel.

## When to push back

- A request to delay a comp adjustment for budget reasons → propose a
  date, don't leave open-ended; people remember being underpaid more
  than they remember being patient.
- An off-the-books arrangement (cash side comp, undocumented
  equity) — refuse; document or don't do it.
- A confidential matter being asked about by another exec — politely
  decline; route through the right channel.
